## What this licence does **not** cover

**Software.** The `src/psychohistory/` package and the `analyses/` and `scripts/` code are under the
MIT licence — see `LICENSE`.

**Data.** No data in or derived from this repository is licensed here.

- *Primary dream reports.* Private. Raw text and user identifiers are not redistributable under any
  terms, and the aggregate tables that are released carry no cell built from fewer than 20 reports or
  5 contributors. See `docs/ETHICS.md`.
- *Third-party corpora.* DreamBank, the Sleep and Dream Database, Reddit-derived corpora and the
  language baselines remain under their providers' terms. Several permit derived aggregates but
  prohibit redistributing raw text; the Wikipedia-derived baseline carries share-alike. Obtain each
  from its original source rather than from here.
- *Indicator series.* FRED, GDELT, EPU, the San Francisco Fed news-sentiment index, Wikimedia,
  Manifold Markets, Google Trends via SerpApi and TMDB each impose their own citation and
  redistribution terms, and two of them may restrict redistribution of fetched series entirely.

One data-card per source in `10-data/manifests/` records the licence, retrieval date, schema and
caveats, and is authoritative where this summary is not.
